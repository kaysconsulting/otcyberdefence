Why it matters now

SOCI has moved from awareness to accountability

Responsible entities must maintain a Critical Infrastructure Risk Management Program that covers cyber and information security hazards, aligned to a recognised framework such as AESCSF, NIST CSF, ISO/IEC 27001 or the Essential Eight. Boards now attest to it every year. We help you get there, and stay there.

01Register critical infrastructure assets and keep ownership and operational information current
02Report significant cyber incidents to the ACSC within tight statutory timeframes
03Adopt, maintain and comply with a written CIRMP, including a recognised cyber framework
04Submit an annual CIRMP report approved by the board or governing body

02 · OT Cyber Protection

Tried, tested and already protecting critical infrastructure worldwide

We bring Australian operators a zero trust OT security platform that is already deployed at more than 5,000 ICS/OT sites across 22 countries, from water districts and energy producers to manufacturers, ports and government facilities. It is purpose-built for industrial environments, protects legacy systems that cannot be patched, and deploys without re-architecting your network or stopping production.

Network cloaking

HMIs, PLCs and RTUs become invisible to scanners and attackers, stopping reconnaissance, the first step in every OT attack chain.

Passwordless MFA

Identity-based access with biometric authentication removes stolen and shared passwords from the equation.

Microsegmentation

Software-defined segments contain any breach and stop lateral movement, without complex firewall rule sets.

Secure remote access

Replace legacy VPNs and jump boxes with zero trust access and recorded sessions for vendors and engineers.
